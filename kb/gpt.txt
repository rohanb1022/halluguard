The Generative Pre-trained Transformer (GPT) series was developed by OpenAI using autoregressive language modeling.
GPT models use only the decoder part of the Transformer architecture.
GPT-3 has 175 billion parameters and was introduced in 2020.
ChatGPT was fine-tuned from the GPT-3.5 series using Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF).
GPT-4 is a large multimodal model capable of processing both image and text inputs.
GPT models generate text by predicting the probability distribution of the next token given preceding tokens.
