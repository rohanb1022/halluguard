An AI hallucination occurs when a large language model generates text that is factually incorrect, nonsensical, or unfaithful to the source.
Intrinsic hallucination refers to generated output that directly contradicts the source or reference information.
Extrinsic hallucination refers to generated output that cannot be verified or grounded from the reference source and is fabricated.
LLM hallucinations are caused by next-token prediction objectives, finite context windows, and knowledge cutoff limits.
Grounding answers with Retrieval-Augmented Generation and claim-level verification significantly reduces hallucination risk.
Human oversight and citation tracking are essential for trustworthy deployment of generative AI systems.
