The Transformer architecture was introduced by Vaswani et al. in the 2017 paper "Attention Is All You Need".
The Transformer relies entirely on self-attention mechanisms without recurrence or convolution.
The original Transformer was evaluated on WMT 2014 English-to-German and English-to-French translation tasks.
The Transformer consists of an encoder stack and a decoder stack each with multi-head attention.
Self-attention allows the model to attend to different positions of the input sequence simultaneously.
The Transformer revolutionized natural language processing by enabling scalable parallel training across modern GPUs.
